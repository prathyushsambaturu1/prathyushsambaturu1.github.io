I'm a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/) at the [University of Oxford](https://www.ox.ac.uk/). I work with [Prof. Michael Wooldridge](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/michael.wooldridge/) and [Prof. Ani Calinescu](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/ani.calinescu/).

My areas of interest include combinatorial optimisation, algorithmic game theory, computational epidemiology, and network science. I am also interested in applications in public health, economics, and climate change.

Earlier, I worked as Postdoctoral Research Assistant in the Department of Biology at the University of Oxford. I have a Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Virginia (UVA) under the guidance of Prof. Anil Vullikanti, with an affiliation to the Biocomplexity Institute and Initiative, UVA. Before that, I completed a Master of Science (MS) by Research in Computer Science at IIIT Hyderabad under the guidance of Prof. Kamalakar Karlapalem and Prof. Kishore Kothapalli.
